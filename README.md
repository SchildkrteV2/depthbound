# Depthbound

A fast top-down roguelike: you are a little ghost diving ever deeper into a generated dungeon.
Devour bosses for their powers, loot gear, send monster parts up the Loot Lift, craft in the
Workshop, and push your Heat level.

## Play

**In your browser:** https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/depthbound/ (works on PC and phones)

Controls (PC): WASD / arrows to move, Space or left mouse to attack (hold the mouse to aim),
Shift or right mouse to dodge, Q/E/R/F for boss powers, Esc to pause.
On phones: on-screen joystick and buttons.

## Build it yourself

Open the project in Godot 4.7, then Project → Export (presets for Web and Windows are included).
